# STEAM Woman Guide

## Purpose

`05-steam-woman.md` integrates a real, verifiable woman in STEAM into each class. The profile connects her work to the class content — it is not a generic biography.

---

## Selection criteria

1. **Relevance to class content** is the primary criterion. Not fame, not alphabetical order.
   - Example: Karen Spärck Jones for a search algorithms class — her work on TF-IDF is the industrial-scale version of that exact problem.
   - Example: Gladys West for a conditional logic class — GPS decision-making uses exactly that type of reasoning.
2. **Verifiability.** Every claim must have a real, accessible source. Do not invent quotes.
3. **Diversity.** Vary by nationality, ethnicity, era, and field across the course. Do not default to the same 3–4 well-known names.
4. **Avoid repetition across Niñas Pro courses.** Consult `references/steam-women-registry.md` before selecting.

---

## Per-profile structure

```
# Mujer STEAM destacada — Clase NN

## [Full Name] ([Birth year]–[Death year or present])

- **Área:** [Field of work]
- **País o contexto:** [Country and era]
- **Relación con la clase:** [1–2 sentences connecting her work to today's content]

### Su trabajo
[Substantive paragraph. Real career facts, real contributions. No vague praise.]

### Frase o idea inspiradora
> "[Quote or paraphrase]"
> — [Name], [source or "paráfrasis de entrevistas documentadas"]

### Pregunta de reflexión
[An open question connecting her experience to the student's own life. Not rhetorical — meant to be discussed.]

### Fuente para verificar
- [Real URL or APA citation]
- [Second source if available]
```

---

## Quote handling

- Use direct quotes only when the source is verifiable (book, interview transcript, documented speech).
- If no direct quote is available, write: `— [Name] (paráfrasis de [entrevistas documentadas / declaraciones públicas])`.
- **Never invent a quote.** Not even a plausible-sounding one.

---

## Integration with other files

The STEAM woman must appear in:
- `01-slides.md`: one dedicated slide in the second half of the lecture.
- `02-kahoot.md`: at least one question about her contribution.

The slide and Kahoot question must be consistent with the profile in `05-steam-woman.md`.
