## Notes

- **Fei-Fei Li, Grace Hopper, and Karen Spärck Jones** appear in multiple courses. For future courses, prioritize women not yet featured.
- **Pensamiento Computacional Básico (2026)** introduced 10 new women to the registry — useful pool for future courses.
- Women may be reused if the course content makes them genuinely the best fit — document the reason in `course-requirements.md`.
- Update this registry after every completed course.
